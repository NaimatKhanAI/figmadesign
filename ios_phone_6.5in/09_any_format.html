The Cyclone
Dorothy lived with her Uncle Henry and Aunt Em in the state of Kansas. They all lived together in a small wooden house in the middle of the vast Kansas prairie. The land was perfectly flat, with no trees or houses nearby.
Uncle Henry was a farmer, and he had built the wooden house himself many years ago. The house had only one room, and that room was used for both eating and sleeping.
There was a small hole under the house called the "cyclone cellar." When fierce cyclone winds blew across the prairie, the family would hide in the cellar.
The scorching sun had burned the color of the little house, turning it gray. The sun had also scorched the ground around the house until everything on the prairie had turned gray. Uncle Henry and Aunt Em's hair had turned gray too, and their faces had become tired and gray as well.
Dorothy's Uncle Henry and Aunt Em were poor and had worked hard all their lives. Being a farmer on the plains was not easy, and Uncle Henry was always very tired. He never smiled, and Aunt Em always looked sad too. But Dorothy always laughed. She had a little black dog named Toto, and she played with Toto all day long.
