There's no single universal way to get rich, but in practice there are some reliable paths people follow. They all come down to the same thing: building assets that keep growing without constantly requiring your time.

The main paths are:

1. Earning more through high-impact skills

This is the most realistic and immediate step.

Focus on skills directly tied to income:

Sales / Negotiation
Marketing (especially performance marketing, ASO/SEO, advertising)
Software / product building
Conversion-focused design
Finance / Analytics

The goal isn't just to get a job, but to become someone whose hourly value is high, because your work creates measurable value.

2. Building something scalable (a business or product)

Wealth usually comes from ownership, not just a salary.

Examples:

Apps (mobile or SaaS)
Agencies that can later be systemized
Digital products (templates, courses, tools)
Content channels that monetize attention
